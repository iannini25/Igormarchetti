Sample launch plan

A Brazilian booking app wins interest from a Polish retailer. What happens next?

Here’s how a software team could use the workshop worksheet. Each milestone has an owner and something the team can show a buyer.

  1. Before making an offer

    Founder

    Compare selling directly with using a Polish reseller. Write down who would sign the contract, issue the invoice and support the retailer.

  2. Before signing the pilot

    Commercial lead

    Ask which security evidence the retailer will accept and whether it requires a certificate. Give the seller, buyer and service details to a tax adviser to check VAT before agreeing the price.

  3. Before taking a live booking

    Product lead

    Trace one customer booking from collection to storage. Record who can see the data, which supplier hosts it and which privacy questions still need an answer.
