# Marchetti Fintech Advisory — design direction

Site: https://lippi.igormarchetti.me (pre-launch preview, `noindex`).

## Audience and job of the page

CROs, COOs, founders and hiring managers in regulated payments and financial services in
Ireland and Europe. The page has one job: make Igor credible in under a minute and start a
conversation on LinkedIn (senior role or defined project).

## Direction

Institutional editorial. A Dublin advisory that reads like a well-set annual report, not a
SaaS landing page.

- **Surfaces:** warm limestone `#f2eee7` (Georgian Dublin stone), never pure white; deep moss
  field `#0e1f1a` for dark sections, never pure black.
- **Accent:** brass `#c2914a` (door furniture on a Georgian door); `#8a5f14` when it carries
  text on paper. Georgian-door green `#1c4c39` for emphasis.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display) + IBM Plex Sans (body) + IBM Plex Mono (labels, register
  numbers). Plex nods to Igor's engineering origins (card-processing platforms, VisionPLUS).
- **Scale:** 1.25 type ratio from 17px; 4px spacing base.
- **Signature:** every content image sits inside a Georgian fanlight arch
  (`border-radius: 50% 50% 3px 3px / 30% 30% 2px 2px`); numbered `01–04` register rules.
- **Imagery:** Dublin docklands, North Wall Quay, a Georgian door, an EMV chip macro, Grand
  Canal Square at dusk. All from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the footer.
- **Motion:** reveal on scroll, scroll progress hairline, scroll-spy nav, light hero parallax
  on pointer devices; transform/opacity only; everything visible under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
