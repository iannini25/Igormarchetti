Privacy and cookies

23 November 2026

Igor Marchetti Lippi runs this website for Lippi in Dublin, Ireland. Contact lippifintech@igormarchetti.me about your data. You can read articles without providing your name or email.

When you contact us

We use your email and enquiry to respond to your request, relying on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries or taking steps you request before a contract. Company survey responses are submitted directly to our private database. The combined survey covers company priorities, pilot interest, training topics, budget, format and language in one submission. It uses no survey-specific tracking cookies. We ask for a company name to count participating organisations, and an optional participant name. We collect a work email only when you agree to a discussion about your responses. We record your choice, its date and the notice version. You can withdraw this permission using the contact address and your reference. This permission does not cover marketing or sales proposals. Only aggregate counts from this survey are intended for AI-assisted research. The site does not send individual submissions to an AI provider. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting engagement or legal obligations.

Newsletter and training

If you opt in, we use your email address and preferred language to send Lippi articles and regulatory updates. When the website confirms signup, our database has saved your address, language, signup and consent dates, and the version and wording of the notice you accepted. If online signup is unavailable, you can try later or send an email request. A draft stays in your email app until you send it. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing lippifintech@igormarchetti.me. We use contact details for updates while consent is current and retain the evidence needed to document consent and respect withdrawals. Permission to discuss survey answers is separate from newsletter subscription.

We use training preferences and founder survey responses to plan services, based on our legitimate interest in understanding demand. Active survey and training responses are subject to routine 90-day cleanup. Please leave personal or confidential details out of free-text answers. Survey themes may be analysed with AI after identifying details are removed.

Browser storage and external services

The site uses local storage named lippi-language for the display language, including the default. It has no automatic expiry. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data settings. Article likes and audience tracking are not currently enabled.

Vercel hosts the website. Hosting and network services process connection information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect them from abuse. Some pages request fonts from Google, which receives the connection information needed to serve them. Our database and email providers process submitted records for the purposes above. Following a Calendly booking link opens Calendly, which processes the booking information you enter there. Contact us for details about providers, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards.

Your choices and rights

Forms are optional. An email address is needed if you want us to send updates or reply. You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email the address above. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority.
