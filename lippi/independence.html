Independence and Conflicts of Interest

Lippi is an independent, project-based advisory practice. Its founder may also have full-time employment. Employment responsibilities take priority, and consultancy assignments are never automatically accepted or approved.

1 · Conflict screening

Every engagement is screened for actual, potential or perceived conflicts with current or future employment, clients, suppliers and other professional commitments.

2 · Employer approval

Any employer approval required by contract or policy must be obtained in writing before work starts. Neither a discovery call nor a proposal guarantees acceptance.

3 · Scope and availability

While the founder holds full-time employment, Lippi does not offer fractional CRO roles or ongoing executive risk management appointments. Training and advisory work are limited to specific, agreed engagements outside employment working hours.

4 · Confidentiality and separation

Lippi does not use employer equipment, confidential information, working time or business relationships for consulting. Where a conflict cannot be managed, the engagement is declined or stopped.

5 · Written engagement terms

Each approved project has written scope, deliverables, fees, timelines and confidentiality provisions. This statement does not override employment contracts or applicable law.
