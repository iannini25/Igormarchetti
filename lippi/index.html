Build the controls the business needs to grow.

Scope of support

Business consultancy for operational decisions.

The proposed services cover management consultancy, operational risk, governance, controls and technology change. They do not include investment advice, arranging financial products, payment services or custody of client money. Legal and tax advice are outside scope.

Clients remain responsible for their regulatory obligations and business decisions. Consultancy support does not replace any required authorisation, regulated appointment or professional advice.
