Market entry & practical readiness

What needs to be clear before you expand?

Start with the services you will provide, who your customers are and how the business will operate. The review turns uncertainties into questions, owners and actions for the destination market.

Entering a European market

Understand the destination requirements

Which permissions or authorised partners could your activities require? What would local governance, customer protection, AML/KYC, data handling and supplier oversight need to cover? For an EU launch, which EU rules could apply? For a UK launch, which UK permissions and controls need review?

Starting point: an operating-model outline, a gap and assumptions register, and an action plan with named owners. We agree the destination country at the start of the project.

Entering Brazil

Understand local operations and partnerships

Which proposed activities raise Banco Central or other local regulatory questions? How will you assess partners, assign responsibilities, handle personal data under LGPD and oversee cross-border operations?

Starting point: documented assumptions, partner due-diligence priorities, a governance outline and questions for Brazilian legal and regulatory specialists.

Lippi helps structure the readiness work and practical controls. Permissions and legal conclusions depend on the specific activities and jurisdiction and require appropriate specialist confirmation.

Example engagements

What a Lippi engagement could look like.

Illustrative scenarios: these hypothetical examples explain potential work and deliverables. They are not client engagements or achieved results.

Fictional example · Entering a European market

A fintech preparing to launch payments in a European market

Situation: the founders plan to launch in Ireland. Their teams keep control evidence in separate documents, and several launch decisions have no clear owner.

Free needs analysis: identify the target service and country, the launch decision, existing permissions assumptions and the most urgent control gaps.

Possible paid project: review the proposed operating model and agreed evidence with the founders and operations lead, then build a risk and control register and a launch checklist.

Deliverables: a documented assumptions register, responsibility map, prioritised action tracker, evidence checklist and questions for local legal or regulatory specialists.

Completion criterion: the team has reviewed the outputs, every agreed priority has an owner, and unresolved launch dependencies are visible. This does not constitute permission to launch.

Fictional example · Entering Brazil

A fintech preparing to enter the Brazilian market

Situation: the COO is assessing a local operating partner ahead of launch. Leadership needs to know which controls each party will own and which gaps could delay entry.

Free needs analysis: clarify the proposed activities, decision date, available partner information and the questions leadership needs answered.

Possible paid project: structure partner due diligence, review the agreed evidence, record gaps and build the governance and monitoring process with the partner owner.

Deliverables: a partner due-diligence checklist, evidence and issues log, responsibility map, draft oversight report and questions for Brazilian specialists.

Completion criterion: the partner owner has reviewed the pack, open issues have owners and dates, and leadership can see the evidence and unresolved dependencies for its decision.

Common questions

Where Lippi comes in.

Do we need training before getting practical help?

Start with the free needs analysis. The main offer is a costed consulting project with practical deliverables. A tailored workshop can help your team use those outputs, but training is optional.

Are you essentially a fractional Chief Risk Officer?

Lippi currently offers defined risk and compliance consulting projects. Regular advisory support can be discussed with a clear scope and time commitment. Fractional CRO and ongoing executive appointments are not currently offered while the founder pursues full-time employment. Individual advisory and training projects require conflict screening and any necessary employer approval.

Irish regulated appointments: official reference ↗

How do you compare with training and certification providers?

Consulting produces the controls, responsibilities, evidence and reports agreed in your project. Training can support the handover. Lippi does not issue PCI DSS certifications or SOC 2 reports. Specialist technical assessment and independent assurance require appropriately qualified providers.

PCI DSS is a payment-card security standard. SOC 2 is an independent examination and report; neither is a general regulation applying to every fintech.

PCI DSS reference ↗ · SOC assurance reference ↗

Can you help us implement a GRC platform?

I can help define the processes, controls, owners and evidence that a platform needs to support, and assess how these fit your existing tools. Any platform configuration, integration or specialist technical work requires its own scope and verified delivery expertise.

What do we get beyond AI-generated advice or a compliance tool?

You work directly with Igor on your business: reviewing agreed evidence, challenging assumptions, building the specified outputs with your team and handing them over to named owners. AI and software can assist the work; the engagement is judged against the practical deliverables and completion criteria in your proposal.

We already have a risk or compliance team. Why bring in Lippi?

You may need capacity for a specific control, partner review or reporting project. I work alongside your team against agreed deliverables, while your leadership retains its decisions and accountability.
