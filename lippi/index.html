What’s included, timing & payment

The €3,000 review. One legal entity, one jurisdiction and one agreed business area. Up to three interviews, agreed documents, one leadership discussion and one feedback round.

Before launch. I review your planned controls and readiness. Testing controls in use needs real operating evidence and a separate agreement.

Timing. Usually two weeks once the documents and people are available. The initial review covers a smaller agreed area and a short gap summary.

Putting the plan into practice. The review includes your plan; carrying out the actions is separate work. Monthly support covers four days, up to 32 hours total. This includes delivery, meetings and agreed email support.

Payment. Fixed fees: 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Monthly support is paid in advance. We agree any extra work or travel costs first.

Other work. Full licence applications, regulator correspondence and extensive policy writing are scoped and priced separately. Legal and tax advice are not included. Support does not guarantee a licence or acceptance by customers.

Before starting, we agree written terms, availability and how to address any conflicts of interest.
