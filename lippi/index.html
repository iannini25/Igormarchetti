Governance, risk and compliance in practice

Lippi provides governance, risk and compliance (GRC) training, platforms and services for technology and financial services companies across Europe, Brazil and the UK.

Our workshops help teams respond to GRC challenges. Teams practise identifying which rules apply when entering a new market, deciding who owns a risk and showing how they know a control works.

Payment providers and companies whose core product is payments technology are outside Lippi’s client scope.
