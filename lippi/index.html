Market entry & practical readiness

What needs to be clear before you expand?

Start with the services you will provide, who your customers are and how the business will operate. The review turns uncertainties into questions, owners and actions for the destination market.

Brazil → EU or UK

Understand the destination requirements

Which permissions or authorised partners could your activities require? What would local governance, customer protection, AML/KYC, data handling and supplier oversight need to cover? Where could DORA, the AI Act or MiCA be relevant?

Starting point: an operating-model outline, market-by-market questions and a readiness action plan. Ireland and Portugal can be explored as EU locations; UK requirements are assessed separately.

EU or UK → Brazil

Understand local operations and partnerships

Which proposed activities raise Banco Central or other local regulatory questions? How will you assess partners, assign responsibilities, handle personal data under LGPD and oversee cross-border operations?

Starting point: documented assumptions, partner due-diligence priorities, a governance outline and questions for Brazilian legal and regulatory specialists.

Lippi helps structure the readiness work and practical controls. Permissions and legal conclusions depend on the specific activities and jurisdiction and require appropriate specialist confirmation.

Common questions

Where Lippi comes in.

Do we need training before getting practical help?

Start where your team is. A workshop is useful when the topic or market is new. If you already understand your requirements, a readiness review or defined advisory project can address the gaps directly.

Are you essentially a fractional Chief Risk Officer?

Lippi currently focuses on training and practical risk advisory. Regular advisory support can be discussed with a clear scope and time commitment. A formal interim or fractional CRO appointment is a separate engagement, subject to suitability, availability and any required approvals.

Irish regulated appointments: official reference ↗

How do you compare with training and certification providers?

Workshops build shared understanding; advisory turns agreed priorities into responsibilities, controls and action plans. Lippi does not issue PCI DSS certifications or SOC 2 reports. Specialist technical assessment and independent assurance require appropriately qualified providers.

PCI DSS is a payment-card security standard. SOC 2 is an independent examination and report; neither is a general regulation applying to every fintech.

PCI DSS reference ↗ · SOC assurance reference ↗

Can you help us implement a GRC platform?

We can start with the processes, responsibilities and requirements a tool needs to support. Platform configuration, integration and technical delivery would need a separate scope and the appropriate delivery expertise.

We already have a risk or compliance team. Why bring in Lippi?

You may need a focused review, a team workshop or support with a specific control, supplier or reporting challenge. I work alongside your team against agreed deliverables. Your leadership retains its decisions and accountability.
